# Skipping Rope · Rope Swings in Arc 🪢

An interactive skipping rope simulation with realistic rope physics, arc swing, and jump timing. Swing the rope and time your jumps!

## Features

- 🪢 **Realistic Rope** – Arc swing with physics
- 🎚️ **Speed Control** – Adjust swing speed (1-10)
- 🎚️ **Jump Control** – Adjust jump height (1-10)
- 🦘 **Jumper** – Animated character with jumps
- 📊 **Jump Counter** – Tracks successful jumps
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume** – Toggle animation
- 🔄 **Start/Stop** – Control rope swinging
- 🔄 **Reset** – Reset everything

## How to Use

1. Click **START** to begin rope swinging
2. Press **Space** to jump
3. Time your jumps to clear the rope
4. Adjust **Speed** for rope swing speed
5. Adjust **Jump** for jump height
6. Click **STOP** to stop the rope
7. Press **RESET** or **R** to reset

## Technical Details

- **Rope Physics:** Arc-based swing with sag
- **Jump Physics:** Gravity with jump power
- **Rendering:** Rope with glow effect
- **Performance:** 60fps with requestAnimationFrame

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Speed Range:** Modify slider range (1-10)
- **Jump Range:** Modify slider range (1-10)
- **Rope Length:** Adjust ropeLength (220)
- **Jumper Size:** Modify `size` (30)
- **Colors:** Adjust rope and character colors
