# 🎆 Light Festival

## Multiple Colored Lights Dance — Mesmerizing Light Show

### Description

**Light Festival** is a breathtaking, mesmerizing light show where dozens of colorful lights dance and swirl in perfect harmony across your screen. Like a festival of fireflies or a spectacular fireworks display, the lights move in beautiful, choreographed patterns — flowing like waves, spinning in circles, bursting outward, spiraling together, pulsing with rhythm, or dancing chaotically. Each light glows with its own vibrant color, leaving soft trails behind as it moves, creating a rich, layered visual experience that's utterly captivating.

The experience is pure visual poetry. Dozens of radiant orbs in every color of the rainbow — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, and more — glide across a dark, starry canvas. Each light pulses gently with its own rhythm, glowing with a warm, ethereal radiance that softens the edges of the dark. The lights don't move randomly — they flow together in beautiful, coordinated patterns that feel alive and organic. Some lights lead, others follow; some spin in wide circles, others weave through the center; some pulse bright and fast, others glow soft and slow. The result is a living, breathing kaleidoscope of color and motion.

But this is more than just a beautiful screensaver — it's a fully interactive light show that you control. Choose from **six distinct patterns** — Wave (flowing waves of light), Circle (spinning rings), Burst (radiant outward pulses), Spiral (mesmerizing spirals), Pulse (heartbeat rhythm), and Random (chaotic dance). Control the **density** to add more lights to the festival or reduce them for a more intimate display. Adjust the **spread** to make the lights dance in a tight cluster or fill the entire screen. Change the **trail** length to create flowing ribbons of light or crisp, clean dots. Switch between **three speeds** — Slow, Medium, or Fast — to match your mood. Pause the show to admire a frozen moment, or reset to start fresh.

Whether you're seeking a mesmerizing screensaver to relax your mind, a colorful visual companion for meditation, a dynamic light show for creative inspiration, or simply a beautiful moment of visual delight, Light Festival delivers with stunning visual quality, hypnotic motion, and intuitive controls that let you shape the celebration of light.

### Features

- 🎆 **Six Dynamic Patterns** — Wave (flowing), Circle (spinning), Burst (radiant), Spiral (mesmerizing), Pulse (rhythmic), Random (chaotic)

- ✨ **Vibrant Color Palette** — 12+ colors including red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, cyan, magenta, and more

- 🌈 **Adjustable Density** — Control the number of lights from 10 to 80 for intimate to spectacular displays

- 💫 **Spread Control** — Adjust movement range from tight cluster to full-screen dance

- 🌟 **Trail Length** — Create flowing ribbons of light or crisp, clean dots

- ⚡ **Three Speed Levels** — Slow (meditative), Medium (balanced), Fast (energetic)

- ⏸️ **Pause & Resume** — Freeze the moment to admire the beautiful light arrangement

- 📊 **Real-Time Status** — Track light count, active pattern, and speed level

- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Control** — Space to pause, R to reset, P for pattern, S for speed, arrow keys for density

- 📱 **Touch & Mouse Support** — Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile

### How to Experience

1. **Open `demo.html`** in any modern browser — no server required

2. **Watch the lights dance** in their beautiful choreographed patterns

3. **Change patterns** with the 🎭 Pattern button or press P

4. **Adjust the density** slider to add or remove lights (10-80)

5. **Adjust the spread** slider to control dance range (20-200)

6. **Adjust the trail** slider to create light ribbons (0-50)

7. **Switch speeds** with the ⚡ Speed button or press S (Slow → Medium → Fast)

8. **Pause the show** with the ⏸️ button or press Space

9. **Reset** with the 🔄 button or press R
